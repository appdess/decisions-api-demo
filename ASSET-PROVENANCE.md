Generated 26 October 2026 using the built-in image generation tool, after Alexander noted that the original abstract SVG did not resemble a pretzel. Saved locally as `pretzel.png`. It is an AI-generated fixture asset, not a photograph of stage props or evidence of model accuracy.

Prompt: “Use case: product-mockup. Create a photorealistic bakery product image for a small educational web demo. Subject: exactly one unmistakable traditional German Laugenbrezel, soft pretzel, seen directly from above. Correct familiar pretzel topology: a broad thick rounded lower belly with a pale split in the crust, two much thinner arms that cross once in the center and attach to the lower curve, exactly three open holes, coarse white salt crystals. Rich golden brown glossy baked crust. Centered on a plain warm beige tabletop (#e8decc approximate), soft natural shadow, entire pretzel visible with generous margins. Landscape 4:3 composition. No plate, no hands, no text, no logo, no watermark, no extra food. This must look like a real German bakery pretzel, not an abstract knot or three interlocked rings.”

Alexander selected **Flag badge**; **Bavarian porcelain** and **Alpine blue** are alternatives. The home cards use system emoji and native/code-drawn symbols. These are UI identity and sample assets, not classification-test photographs or evidence of model accuracy.
